## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes the financial performance of **TechNova Global Electronics Ltd.**, a fictional multinational electronics manufacturer operating across multiple regions, customer segments, industries, and product categories.

Despite maintaining healthy profit margins and expanding geographically, the company experienced declining revenue over recent years. The objective of this project was to identify the root causes of the decline through data analysis and deliver actionable business recommendations using SQL, Python, and Power BI.

---

# 🎯 Business Problem

TechNova's executive leadership observed a continuous decline in annual revenue despite:

- Stable pricing strategy
- Consistent profitability
- Product availability

Management wanted to determine whether the decline was caused by:

- Regional underperformance

# 📊 Key Business Insights

## Revenue Analysis

- Revenue declined across most business dimensions during **2025**.
- The decline was concentrated in **February** and **July–October**, indicating seasonal or operational challenges rather than a continuous downward trend.
- **Europe** experienced the largest regional decline, while **North America** remained the highest revenue contributor.
- **Government** was the only customer segment to record positive growth.
- **IT** was the only industry with positive YoY growth.
- **Networking**, **Storage**, and **Monitor** products contributed most to revenue decline.
- Revenue recovered during **September**, **November**, and **December**, suggesting opportunities to replicate successful strategies.

---

## Order Volume Analysis

- Order volume decreased by **2.05% YoY**, confirming that declining customer demand—not pricing—was the primary revenue driver.
- Average Order Value remained almost unchanged (**+0.07%**), indicating stable customer spending.
- **Networking** products experienced the largest reduction in orders.
- **Europe** and **South America** recorded the greatest regional order losses.
- **SMB** and **Retail** customers accounted for most of the decline.
- **Software**, **Enterprise**, **USA**, **Saudi Arabia**, and **Argentina** demonstrated positive order growth.

---

## Customer Churn Analysis

- Overall customer churn rate reached **2.33%**.
- **114 customers** were lost.
- Estimated revenue loss from churn exceeded **$9.35M**.
- **Retail** customers experienced the highest churn.
- The **USA**, **Argentina**, and **Brazil** recorded the highest number of churned customers.
- A small number of high-value customers accounted for a significant share of lost revenue.

# 📌 Business Conclusion

The analysis shows that TechNova's declining revenue is primarily a **demand-generation challenge rather than a pricing or profitability issue**. Stable profit margins and Average Order Value indicate that customers who continued purchasing maintained similar spending behavior. However, declining order volume, weaker performance across specific markets, customer segments, industries, and product categories, together with the loss of several high-value customers, contributed to the revenue slowdown.

By focusing on customer acquisition, strategic retention, regional recovery, and product portfolio optimization, TechNova can reverse its declining revenue trend and establish a sustainable path for long-term growth.
